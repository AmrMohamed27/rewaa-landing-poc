# Rewaa Education Platform (منصة رِواء التعليمية)

## Product Overview
- **Name:** Rewaa Educational Platform (منصة رِواء التعليمية)
- **Role:** Full-Stack Architect
- **Description:** Multi-tenant educational management platform connecting center managers, teachers, assistants, and students with robust course authoring, exam management, and payment verification workflows.
- **Landing Page Target Audience:** **Students (الطلاب)** — The landing page is tailored directly towards students to help them discover top teachers, enroll in premium courses & individual lessons, take interactive exams, and easily track approvals and learning progress.
- **Technologies:** Next.js (App Router), Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, VPS, GitHub Actions.

## Target Roles & Core Capabilities

### 1. Students (الطلاب) — [Primary Landing Page Focus]
- **Course & Lesson Access:** Seamless browsing, enrollment, and access to top teacher courses, recorded modular lessons, and downloadable materials.
- **Flexible Purchasing & Payments:** Purchase complete courses or individual lessons/exams with convenient local payment methods (Vodafone Cash, Fawry, InstaPay, Bank transfers).
- **Payment Proof Upload:** Upload bank transfers and e-wallet receipts with fast verification and real-time enrollment approval tracking.
- **Interactive Learning & Assessment:** Take interactive quizzes, timed mock exams with automated scoring, and view instant answer explanations to boost academic grades.
- **Progress Tracking:** Clear dashboard tracking completed lessons, upcoming exams, and performance statistics.

### 2. Teachers & Teaching Assistants (المعلمون والمساعدون)
- **Course & Lesson Management:** Full capabilities for hosting courses, modular video content, and downloadable materials.
- **Exam & Assessment Engine:** Comprehensive question banks, quizzes, automated scoring, and student progress tracking.

### 3. Educational Center Managers (مديرو المراكز التعليمية)
- **Staff & Center Operations:** Manage teachers, assistants, financial analytics, and verify student payment receipts.
